SLAO Fabric Care Guide

Follow these care instructions to maintain the color vibrancy, embroidery, and softness of our premium fabrics & original designer prints.

Embroidered Lawn & Cotton

  • Hand wash separately in cold water using a mild liquid detergent.
  • Do not soak for prolonged periods or use harsh bleach/stain removers.
  • Dry inside-out in shade to protect original designer prints from direct sunlight.
  • Iron on medium heat from the reverse side, especially over zari or thread embroidery.

Chiffon, Organza & Festive Wear

  • Dry clean only to preserve delicate handwork, embellishments, and fabric drape.
  • Avoid wringing or twisting wet chiffon/organza garments.
  • Use steam ironing on low setting with a protective pressing cloth.
  • Store folded in breathable muslin or cotton garment bags away from moisture.

Jacquard, Silk & Linen Co-Ords

  • Gentle cold hand wash or professional dry clean recommended for silk blends.
  • Do not tumble dry; lay flat or hang on padded hangers to prevent stretching.
  • Avoid spraying perfumes or deodorants directly onto silk or jacquard weaves.

Pre-Stitching Tip (Unstitched)

  • Soak unstitched cotton/lawn fabric in plain cold water for 20–30 minutes before tailoring to allow natural shrinkage.
  • Dry completely in shade and press gently before cutting.
